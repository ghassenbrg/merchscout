# Niche Scoring Rubric

Final opportunity score:

```txt
Demand signal                 25%
Low-to-medium saturation       20%
Product fit                   15%
Marketplace fit               10%
Search keyword quality         10%
Design originality potential   10%
Compliance/IP safety           10%
```

Reject candidates automatically when:

- a known brand, franchise, public figure, team, or character is central,
- trademark risk is high,
- the design depends on copyrighted slogans or lyrics,
- the concept is fan art,
- the joke only works because of protected IP,
- content risks Amazon rejection,
- the design cannot be made original enough.

Scoring notes:

- Demand can come from evergreen fit, seasonality, regional fit, trend notes, or external adapters.
- Saturation should penalize obvious broad niches and reward specific cross-niches.
- Compliance is a hard gate before final selection.
