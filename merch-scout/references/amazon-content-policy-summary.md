# Amazon Content Policy Summary

This is a working lint checklist, not legal advice. Merch Scout must never claim a design is legally safe.

Hard rejection categories:

- brand, franchise, logo, character, fan art, or team references without rights,
- celebrity, public figure, or name/likeness usage without rights,
- copyrighted lyrics, quotes, album art, posters, memes, or slogans,
- hateful, violent, sexually explicit, illegal, misleading, or medical-claim content,
- misleading words such as official, licensed, guaranteed, or best seller,
- keyword stuffing or unrelated search terms.

Required wording:

```txt
No obvious trademark conflict found in checked sources.
This is not legal advice.
Human review is required before upload.
```

Human review must verify:

- visual originality,
- spelling and localization,
- trademark/IP checks in the target marketplaces,
- Amazon account-specific product availability,
- current upload UI field limits,
- policy-sensitive context not detectable by scripts.
